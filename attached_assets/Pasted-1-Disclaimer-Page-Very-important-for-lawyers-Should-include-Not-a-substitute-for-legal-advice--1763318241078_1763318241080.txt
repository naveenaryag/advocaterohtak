1. Disclaimer Page

Very important for lawyers.
Should include:

Not a substitute for legal advice

No attorney–client relationship unless formally agreed

Website content is for information only

Jurisdiction applicability

No guarantee of case results

Google reads disclaimers for trust signals.

2. Privacy Policy

Must disclose:

What data you collect (forms, Cookies, Analytics, etc.)

How you store client data

Third-party tools

Data protection compliance

Contact email for grievances

Google expects this on all business websites.

3. Terms & Conditions

Should include:

Conditions for using the website

Intellectual property rights

Limitations of liability

User responsibilities

Governing law (Haryana jurisdiction)

Shows legal maturity & professionalism.

4. Refund & Cancellation Policy (Optional for Lawyers)

If you allow online consultation booking, add this.

Even if you don’t, add a line saying:

“Legal consultation fees are non-refundable once advice is provided.”

5. Cookie Policy (If using Analytics or Meta Pixel)

Required if:

You use Google Analytics

Facebook Pixel

Hotjar

Any tracking cookies

6. “No Attorney–Client Relationship” Page (Separate Page or Part of Disclaimer)

This is a required compliance page for law websites in many jurisdictions.

Should clearly say:

“Submitting a form or contacting us does not create an advocate–client relationship.”

7. ‘Legal Services Compliance’ Page

This is optional but highly boosts EEAT.

Should include:

Bar Council of India rules you follow

Your Bar Enrollment Number

Ethics compliance statement

Types of cases you accept & do not accept

Consumer protection disclaimers

Huge trust booster.

8. Grievance Redressal Contact

Add a dedicated section (part of Privacy Policy or separate):

Grievance Officer

Email address

Postal address

Response time (like 48–72 hrs)

Google considers this as part of "Trust".

🏛️ Highly Recommended Pages (Help Ranking & EEAT)
9. About the Advocate

Include:

Bar Council Enrollment number

Years of experience

Courts you practice in

Awards, qualifications, achievements

Publications

Speaking engagements

This is one of the strongest EEAT pages.

10. Contact Page with Multiple Validations

Must include:

Mobile number

Working WhatsApp number

Office address

Google Map embed

Email

Consultation form

11. Client Testimonials (with Proof)

Google values reviews on:

Google My Business

Client signed reviews (never use fake reviews)

Make sure the page is:

Short

Authentic

With initials (not full names) for confidentiality

12. Case Results Page (Optional)

You cannot list client names without consent.
So write in this style:

“Successfully obtained anticipatory bail for a client in a false FIR (2023).”

“Helped a family settle a cheque bounce matter out of court (2024).”

These generic success summaries do NOT violate ethics.
