How a Supreme Court Case Works – Step-by-Step

Most people do not understand the actual Supreme Court process. Here is a clear guide:

1. Case Evaluation & Legal Opinion

We examine whether the matter is fit for the Supreme Court.

2. Drafting of SLP / Appeal Petition

Strong drafting = higher chances of admission.

3. Filing Through an Advocate-on-Record (AOR)

Every Supreme Court filing must be signed and filed by an AOR.

4. Listing Before Supreme Court Bench

Date is given through the automated listing system.

5. Arguments on Admission

Court decides whether to issue notice or dismiss.

6. Final Hearings & Disposal

Once admitted, case proceeds on merits.
